Pharmacy Clinical Stockout Control System

A rigorous Systems Analysis and Design framework targeting clinical supply chains, proactive stockout prevention triggers, and fault-tolerant medical inventory tracking.

ROLE Systems Analyst
CONTEXT S&D Architectural Blueprint
MODELING UML • State Machines • ERD
KEY RESULT Zero-Stockout Thresholds

The Supply Chain & Clinical Threat

In healthcare facilities, stockouts of essential pharmaceuticals lead directly to interrupted clinical care. Traditional paper-based or uncoordinated inventory spreadsheets result in unnoticed stock depletions, untracked batch expirations, and uncoordinated emergency orders.

Component Specifications & Actor Boundaries

The specification establishes strict validation tiers to govern the entire life cycle of medical inventory from warehouse ingestion to clinical dispensation:

  • Actor Matrix Boundaries: Differentiates permissions across Pharmacists (dispensation records), Inventory Managers (threshold approval), and Administrators (audit compliance).
  • Automated Reorder Thresholds: Triggers procurement alerts the instant unit volume falls beneath the safety buffer stock level.
  • Batch Expiration Quarantine: Automatically flags approaching expiration dates to prevent distributing compromised pharmaceuticals.

Engineering Blueprint Deliverables

Produced complete UML use-case models, entity-relationship diagrams (ERDs), and state chart specifications providing engineering teams with a clear roadmap for full-stack implementation.
